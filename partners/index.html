A CONVERSATION WITH A PURPOSE

Start small.
Make it useful.

These are possible collaboration formats, not active partnerships or guaranteed opportunities.

  1. Find the overlap

    What do your learners need? What real context or experience can you bring?

  2. Define the experience

    Agree on a focused brief, support, timeline, and what a useful output looks like.

  3. Learn from the work

    Review the experience and the evidence before deciding what should come next.
