PRIVACY & SITE INFORMATION

A clear look
at this early website.

This page explains how the current preview works. A full company privacy notice and relevant enrolment terms must be provided before a public launch.

Enquiry drafts

The enquiry form currently prepares a draft in your browser. It does not send the information to ZelSpark or save it in a company database. Reloading or leaving the page clears the draft. If you download it, the file is saved on your device and is yours to manage.

Mini challenges

The studio checklist is temporary page state. It is not a student record, assessment, or certified completion. No account, progress dashboard, or LMS is connected.

Cookies and analytics

This website’s application code does not set advertising cookies, use local storage, or include marketing analytics. The hosting platform may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, and the private preview may require host-managed authentication.

Content and availability

Learning paths, modules, projects, and collaboration formats are illustrative concepts. Program dates, delivery modes, eligibility, fees, mentors, certificates, internships, and placements are not confirmed. The studio photograph is AI-generated and does not depict actual ZelSpark students.

Before launch

ZelSpark’s legal entity, official contact channel, data controller details, retention rules, service providers, applicable learner or guardian consent process, and rights request process must be confirmed. Privacy, terms, and any cancellation or refund policies must then reflect the real service.
