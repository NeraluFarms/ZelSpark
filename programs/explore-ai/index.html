SAMPLE CONCEPT

This outline shows a possible learning experience. Curriculum and outcomes are illustrative, not a confirmed offer.

What you could learn to do

  1. 01Describe a useful task and set a clear success criterion.
  2. 02Compare different prompts against the same task.
  3. 03Check an AI output against a trusted source.
  4. 04Document risks, limitations, and where human review is needed.

The path from idea to output

01Know the tool+

Explore capabilities, common failure modes, and the difference between a fluent answer and a reliable one.

02Design an experiment+

Write a task, choose a test input, and compare two prompt approaches.

03Evaluate the result+

Check accuracy, relevance, and uncertainty. Avoid using private or sensitive information.

04Build a responsible workflow+

Combine a tool with human checks and explain when the workflow should not be used.

YOUR POSSIBLE PROJECT

A tested AI-assisted study workflow

Design a workflow that turns your own non-sensitive study notes into revision questions. Test the questions for accuracy and usefulness, and document the human checks.

A starting point, not a label

Suggested starting point: interest in AI and a willingness to question outputs. Tool access, age requirements, and eligibility will be confirmed before enrolment.

How learning could be reviewed

The proposed approach combines a working output, a short explanation of your choices, and reflection on what you would improve. Assessment criteria, mentor support, and certificate terms are not yet confirmed.

Before you take the next step

Can I enrol in a program now?+

Not yet. The learning paths on this website are sample concepts. Enrolment dates, confirmed curriculum, fees, and delivery details will be announced once they are ready.

Will learning be online or in person?+

Delivery mode, schedule, duration, and access requirements are still being developed. These will be published for each program before anyone enrols.

Will there be mentors, certificates, or internships?+

Mentorship and real-world exposure are part of the vision. No mentors, certificates, internships, placements, or employment outcomes are confirmed or promised on this preview website. Any future offering will have its own clear terms.
